Language & fonts

Make Burmese text readable, portable and consistent across devices.

MM/01
ကTHE STANDARD

Pyidaungsu Font

Pyidaungsu is the official national standard Unicode font for Myanmar text. Unicode text stores characters consistently, so it can be searched, copied and shared across compatible apps and devices.

Font & resources at MCF ↗
UnicodeCross-platformMyanmar script
≋WHY IT MATTERS

Zawgyi-One vs Unicode

Zawgyi is a legacy, non-standard encoding. It may look correct on a device that has Zawgyi installed but appear out of order or unreadable elsewhere. Unicode is the recommended choice for new documents and digital publishing.

Legacy · Zawgyi→Standard · Unicode

04 / BUY, BUILD, MAINTAIN

Hardware & market guide

Make a plan before you shop. Compare the complete setup, not just a headline price.

MM/04
01LAPTOP BUYING

Choose for the work

For study and office work, compare a recent processor, 16 GB memory if the budget allows, SSD capacity, display quality and battery life. For design or gaming, check the dedicated GPU and cooling.

  • Confirm exact model number and condition
  • Ask about warranty coverage and service location
  • Test keyboard, screen, ports, Wi-Fi and battery
  • Compare total price with OS and accessories included
